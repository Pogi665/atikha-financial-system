# 009: Manual evidence authority and assistive extraction

- Status: Active
- Selected date: Recorded by October 6, 2026; approved Stage 7/redesign contracts refine presentation without changing financial authority.
- Source/evidence: [Legacy decisions](../history/project-decisions-legacy.md), [combined master](../stages4-7-plan.md), [redesign plan](../presentation-redesign-plan.md).

## Decision

Original images, manual review, accepted/excluded totals, allocations and server gross coverage remain authoritative. OCR/provider/item suggestions cannot automatically verify documents or post financial records. Ordinary evidence may be optional; attached documents require truthful review/coverage. Informational proof supplies no monetary expenditure coverage. Preserve ownership/reservations, posted associations and separately validated correction-lineage reuse; unused/excluded posted-image portions cannot support another partial liquidation.

Scan Receipt is Upload -> Review Details -> Continue to General Journal, its actual handoff. Verification alone is not a financial posting. Existing schema/feature gates and Management evidence restrictions also apply through legacy/flags-off paths.

## Reason

Extraction assists staff but does not establish authenticity, eligibility or accounting truth.

## Supersedes

Treating scan/verification as automatic payment/receipt/posting or provider suggestions as reviewed support is a superseded interpretation, not a claimed former client policy.

## Does not change

Gross coverage, exact totals, original bytes, privacy, explicit return proof, correction eligibility or provider configuration/call authorization.

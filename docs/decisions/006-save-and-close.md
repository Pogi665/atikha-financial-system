# 006: One explicit Save and close in focused entry

- Status: Active
- Selected date: October 22, 2026 actual-human selection; reaffirmed by October 24 redesign amendments.
- Source/evidence: The explicit U5 selection in [legacy decisions](../history/project-decisions-legacy.md) and binding [redesign amendments](../presentation-redesign-plan.md).

## Decision

Focused ordinary/advance/correction entry has one explicit Save and close action. Persist the unfinished owner-private draft and return to the appropriate My Drafts only after valid acknowledged saving. Failed, malformed or uncertain saves preserve populated input and route, without blind replay. Review may save internally; Review is not posting. Confirm/Record remains the separate financial action.

Budget Draft and Reporting Setup retain their own approved save/review/submit semantics; visual consistency must not replace their contracts.

## Reason

The user found separate Save draft and Save and close redundant and explicitly retained Save and close.

## Supersedes

**Superseded:** the earlier two-visible-save-control choice and incidental concept wording proposing stay-on-page Save Draft for focused entry.

## Does not change

Private ownership, validation, financial confirmation, durable identities or legitimate distinct saving behavior in other workflows.

# 003: Immutable posted financial and evidence history

- Status: Active
- Selected date: Recorded by October 6, 2026; visit notes and subsequent approved stage contracts provide provenance.
- Source/evidence: [Client evidence](../client-context.md), [legacy decisions](../history/project-decisions-legacy.md), [Stage 3 plan](../stage3-plan.md).

## Decision

Preserve posted journals, original evidence bytes/associations, captured labels, frozen reviewed snapshots and audit history. Worksheet-like presentation never makes posted cells editable. Financial changes use authorized linked corrections; draft editing or current master names do not overwrite historical labels or frozen revisions.

## Reason

Corrections must be traceable without making original history appear to have been different.

## Supersedes

The interpretation of adjustable entry as direct edit/delete of a posted original is superseded; original note wording remains archived.

## Does not change

Legitimate draft/configuration editing, audited current-master maintenance, exact readers or correction eligibility.

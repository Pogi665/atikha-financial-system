# 012: Effective approved budget revisions and recognized actuals

- Status: Active
- Selected date: October 23, 2026 combined plan/authorization; retained by October 24 redesign.
- Source/evidence: [Master plan](../stages4-7-plan.md), [plan approval](../stages4-7-plan-approval.md), [budget service](../../includes/budget_revisions.php), [legacy decisions](../history/project-decisions-legacy.md).

## Decision

Draft, Submitted, Returned, Approved and superseded Approved revisions are distinct. Management decides the exact submitted revision. A new draft/submission does not replace the current effective approved budget, visually or financially. The server-approved effective revision is authoritative; legacy Budgets remain separately approval-unverified and are never automatically promoted.

Only legitimate draft allocation cells are editable. Preserve blank/no allocation versus explicit zero. Review is read only before explicit submission/approval. Actuals use signed recognized expense lines, not payment-book totals, with authoritative project tags. Frozen reports capture the exact approved basis or explicit absence of approval instead of inventing a budget.

The October 29 final pre-defense presentation uses explicit expense-account/project/amount rows with duplicate allocation validation. [Suggested Budget Baseline](013-deterministic-planning-and-audit-advisories.md) creates only an explicitly requested private draft from selected eligible server-verified estimates; it does not create approval or replace the effective revision.

The October 29 owner correction sets current-month actuals through the current accounting date and past-month actuals through month-end. Future budgets display zero actual activity with no claimed actual cutoff; approved allocations remain available. A forecast-derived revision copies the effective approved allocation context, overlays only selected positive recommendations, and preserves unselected allocations, including deliberate existing zeroes.

## Reason

Planning revisions and posted actuals have different authority; pending submission must not erase the effective approval.

## Supersedes

Implicit promotion of legacy rows/pending submissions to effective approval and equating payment totals with recognized expenses are superseded interpretations, not confirmed historical client policy.

## Does not change

Reviewer authority, blank/zero meaning, exact submission identity, financial history, client allocation policy or approval/audit transaction rules.

3. Prepare a receipt, including an optional image. Find More Options, return to the simple form without losing content, and recover from a deliberately missing required field.
4. Release a synthetic advance with an explicitly selected control account. Liquidate the used portion and return the remainder with reviewed proof. Explain Released / Liquidated / Returned / Outstanding and the difference between Partial and Overdue.
5. Find a transaction by journal identity. Open the General Ledger, change period, search one movement and explain why full-period opening/ending balances remain. Export matching rows and print the full period; identify each scope.
6. Prepare a correction. Explain Original Transaction / Reversal / Corrected Transaction and why reversal is not an actual cash return. Check the immutable original and exact result links.
7. Open Reports. Identify Working Figures versus an exact Submitted Copy. Switch primary reports and Accounting Details. Find a setup blocker and its separate Admin setup task without inventing a classification.
8. Prepare a budget draft with both blank and explicit-zero cells. Save/resume, Review Budget and inspect the read-only proposed grid against current effective approval. Submit for Approval. Explain why submission does not replace the existing approval.
9. Find a project/party editor, Reporting Setup, Chart of Accounts and User Management. Explain which information is editable versus protected. Inspect validation, keyboard focus and dismissal without losing a populated form.
10. Scan a visibly synthetic image with provider calls disabled. Explain assistive suggestions, manual confirmation and Continue to General Journal. No document action itself posts money.
11. Open Email, New Email and Sent. Inspect fake Failed / Sending / Delivery Unconfirmed examples. Explain why an uncertain delivery should not be blindly resent. Locate a read-only historical communications item without creating Board messages.
